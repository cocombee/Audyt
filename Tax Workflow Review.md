# Audyt tax workflow review

Audyt's Taxes route is currently a layout preview. Its tabs and filters do not read accounting records, and there is no tax registration, return, filing, or tax-payment record. This is a product gap, not a zero-balance tax report.

## Reference workflow

Zoho Books separates tax-rate setup from the return workflow. Its return settings identify the reporting period, start date, and tax basis. A user generates a return for a period, opens its figures to inspect the underlying transactions, records adjustments with a reason, marks the return as filed **after** filing with the authority, and records a payment against that return. Its list distinguishes unfiled returns from filing history. The relevant controls are **New Tax Rate**, **Generate Return**, **Adjust**, **Mark as Filed**, **Record Payment**, **Payment History**, and **Export**. See [Zoho tax settings](https://www.zoho.com/ca/books/help/settings/taxes.html), [return workflow](https://www.zoho.com/ca/books/help/canada-tax/gst-hst-return.html), and [tax payments](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/accountant/tax-payment-adjustment.html). The Canadian and US editions illustrate the interaction pattern; their tax rules must not be copied into Malaysia.

For Malaysia, MySST says the ordinary SST taxable period is two months, returns are required even when no tax is payable, and the return is due by the last day of the following month. Sales tax and service tax are declared separately on SST-02, and filing is done through MySST. See [MySST filing](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/filing-text-returns/), [SST-02 form](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/assets/document/SST%20Form/SST-02%20%28Latest%20Release%29.pdf), and the [sales](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/faq-sales-tax/) and [service](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/faq-services-tax/) tax FAQs. These are starting rules; an approved different period and transaction-specific treatment must be represented explicitly.

## Audyt implementation contract

1. **Registration and codes.** Store the company's sales-tax and/or service-tax registration, effective dates, registration number, taxable-period start, approved period variation, tax codes/rates, exemptions, and a versioned effective date for each rate. Preserve historical transaction rates rather than rewriting posted entries when a code changes.
2. **Transaction evidence.** Give invoice and credit-note lines a tax-code identity, taxable base, tax amount, and treatment. Add equivalent explicit treatment and amount to purchases and imported services where applicable. Keep posted documents and their journal entries linked. Audyt already stores sales-line tax amounts and a tax-liability posting account, but does not store a complete tax-code identity; expense `tax` is currently a category string, not a return-ready tax amount.
3. **Review.** Build a period report from posted source documents and their reversals, separated by sales tax and service tax. Show the source rows behind every amount, exclusions with reasons, unmatched tax journals, late entries into prior periods, and a reconciliation to the liability accounts. Never use a generic expense category or a bank balance as a tax figure.
4. **Return lifecycle.** Create an immutable generated snapshot with its covered period, included source IDs, rates, totals, preparer, and generation time. Support review adjustments as separate traceable records. Allow a filed status only after the user records the actual MySST filing date and reference; the button must say **Mark as Filed**, not **File to MySST**, because Audyt has no filing integration. A filed period needs correction/amendment controls rather than silent regeneration.
5. **Payment.** Record a full or partial tax payment against one filed return, with a real payment account, payment date, reference, and posted clearing journal. Reconcile it to a bank statement and show payment history and remaining due. Do not infer payment from a return's filed status.
6. **Presentation.** Replace the preview tabs with **Overview**, **Tax Codes**, **Returns**, and **Payments**. The overview should show a configured/unconfigured state, the current taxable period, source-backed tax totals, exceptions, due date, and the next action. A return detail should provide **Review Transactions**, **Adjust**, **Export**, **Mark as Filed**, and then **Record Payment** as state permits. Show explicit “not configured” or “no posted tax transactions” states rather than invented percentages or RM amounts.

## Build order and release gate

Implement the persistence and tax-source contracts first, then transaction capture and reversals, then period calculation, return snapshots, payment posting, and finally the UI. Test rate changes across dates, zero returns, credits, late prior-period transactions, sales versus service tax, payment partials, and the filed-period edit boundary. Compare a generated SST-02 worksheet to the current official form before presenting any amount as filing-ready. Until those checks pass, keep the current Taxes page labelled as a preview and do not present it as a live return.
